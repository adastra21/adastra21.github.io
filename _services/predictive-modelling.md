## Predictive Modelling

Turn your historical data into forward-looking insights. I build practical models that integrate into your existing workflows — not academic exercises that sit in a notebook.

### What's Included

- **Demand Forecasting** — Predict inventory needs, traffic spikes, and revenue trajectories
- **Churn Prediction** — Identify at-risk customers before they leave and quantify intervention impact
- **Customer Segmentation** — Cluster users by behavior, value, and lifecycle stage for targeted action
- **NLP & Text Analysis** — Extract insights from reviews, support tickets, and survey responses

### Who It's For

Product and growth teams that have data but lack the modelling expertise to extract predictions from it.
