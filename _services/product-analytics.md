## Product Analytics

Understanding how users interact with your product is the fastest path to better retention and growth. I help teams move from gut feelings to data-backed product decisions.

### What's Included

- **Event Tracking Design** — Define a clean taxonomy so every click, view, and action tells a story
- **Funnel & Retention Analysis** — Find where users drop off and what keeps them coming back
- **Cohort & Segmentation** — Break users into meaningful groups to target improvements where they matter most
- **Self-Serve Reporting** — Set up tools and queries your PM and engineering teams can run on their own

### Who It's For

Product teams that have outgrown basic page-view analytics and need deeper behavioural insight to guide roadmap decisions.
