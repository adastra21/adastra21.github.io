## Data Strategy

Getting analytics right from day one saves months of confusion down the road. I work with startups to build a data foundation that scales with the business.

### What's Included

- **Core Metric Definition** — Identify the metrics that actually drive your business, not vanity numbers
- **Dashboard Design** — Build dashboards your team will check daily, not once and forget
- **Experimentation Frameworks** — Set up A/B testing and feature flagging so you can ship with confidence
- **Data Infrastructure Review** — Audit your current stack and recommend what to keep, replace, or add

### Who It's For

Early-stage startups (seed to Series B) building their first data function, or growing companies whose analytics have fallen behind their product.
