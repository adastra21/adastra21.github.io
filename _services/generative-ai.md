## Generative AI

Generative AI is moving fast — most teams are experimenting but few are shipping reliably. We help startups cut through the hype and build practical AI systems that automate meaningful work, integrate cleanly with existing tools, and behave predictably in production.

### What's Included

- **Agentic Workflow Design** — Identify tasks suited for automation and architect reliable multi-step AI agents
- **MCP Server Development** — Build and deploy Model Context Protocol servers to extend LLM capabilities with your own data and tools
- **LLM Integration** — Connect language models to your product stack via API, with structured outputs, guardrails, and fallbacks
- **Evaluation & Red Teaming** — Test model behaviour against edge cases, adversarial inputs, and failure modes before going to production

### Who It's For

Product and engineering teams that want to move beyond demos and ship AI features that actually hold up — without building a dedicated AI team from scratch.
