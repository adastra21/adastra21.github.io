## Responsible AI

As AI becomes central to product decisions, the stakes of getting it wrong increase. I help teams build AI systems that are fair, transparent, and auditable.

### What's Included

- **Bias Auditing** — Test models across demographic groups and identify disparate impact
- **Fairness-Aware Preprocessing** — Apply techniques to mitigate bias without sacrificing performance
- **Model Explainability** — Make black-box models interpretable for stakeholders and regulators
- **AI Ethics Review** — Evaluate your ML pipeline against emerging responsible AI frameworks

### Who It's For

Companies deploying ML models in high-stakes domains — lending, hiring, insurance, healthcare — or anyone who wants to ship AI they can stand behind.
